Logo usage and geometry:
The current official header SVG is the compact original UXUY wordmark, viewBox 0 0 106 40. Two U letter forms and Y are white; the original three-piece X is #FE4200. Its seven original paths are retained exactly, not recreated with text fonts.
The official brand archive provides white and black wordmark variants with orange X in a larger, padded viewBox 0 0 655 264, plus black-background square/circle original X symbols. Both compact website mark and padded archive wordmarks are official sources; choose one without mixing/retyping their geometry.
No scripts, event handlers, external references or foreignObject exist in the downloaded SVGs. No recolouring, trimming, rescaling of path coordinates or geometry changes were performed; downloaded files are byte-identical to their official sources.

Rights and use:
UXUY retains trademark and copyright rights; these are official brand assets, not an MIT or CC0 asset license. The official documentation permits original logo use to refer to/link to UXUY products and says official-partnership announcements require approval. It requires maintaining original proportions, using original colours (or approved monochrome where needed), clear space and legibility, and avoiding implied endorsement or using the mark as an unrelated product's identity.
